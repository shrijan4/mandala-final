Our Story

Every Piece Carries a Name, a Family, a Place

Why Mandala

Started With a Simple Idea

Mandala began with a simple belief: the handicraft traditions of Nepal — singing bowl casting, wood carving, brass repoussé — deserve to be seen and owned outside the markets of Kathmandu and Bhaktapur, without losing what makes them meaningful.

We work directly with artisan families, many practicing techniques passed down for generations. There's no warehouse full of identical stock — most pieces are made after you order, then shipped straight from Nepal to wherever you call home.

Direct From Nepal

What "Shipped From Nepal" Means

Rather than importing pieces in bulk, we ship each order directly from Nepal once it's ready. It takes a little longer to reach you — usually 8 to 14 days — but it means fresher stock, fairer pay for artisans, and a shorter path between the workshop and your shelf.
